# CAD

Mechanical design files for the REFLEX enclosure.

## Design Tool

Autodesk Fusion 360

## Printable Parts

STL exports are stored in [stl/](stl/):

- **REFLEX_Shell.stl** - main enclosure body
- **REFLEX_Cover.stl** - removable cover
- **REFLEX_Coupon.stl** - small fit/tolerance test print

## Design Requirements

The enclosure was designed around the Arduino Mega 2560, custom perfboard, 16x2 LCD, four 30 mm illuminated arcade buttons, an internal slim USB power bank, and a short USB-A to USB-B power cable.

The layout prioritizes service access and keeps the Mega, perfboard, and battery from being stacked directly on top of one another.

The enclosure has been submitted for 3D printing; final fit and full-system assembly are still pending.
